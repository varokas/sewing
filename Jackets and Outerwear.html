Jackets and Outerwear

Blazers, coats, and anoraks — the deep end. Everything from the other guides shows up here, plus structure (interfacing strategy), the notched collar, and the bagged lining. Work up to this via Tops and Shirts (collars, set-in sleeves) and Dresses (linings). Part of the Sewing Manual.

Fabric

First jacket

  • Linen or cotton twill — unlined / half-lined blazer
  • Or a boxy wool coat
  • Avoid slippery or pinstriped fabric

Classic

  • Wool flannel
  • Tweed
  • Melton
  • Coating wool

Casual outerwear

  • Waxed canvas
  • Ripstop
  • Twill (anorak / chore-coat)

Lining

  • Bemberg rayon for blazers
  • Flannel-backed or quilted lining for warmth

Assembly Order — Lined Blazer (Speed-Tailoring Method)

1Interface

This is the big decision layer. Block-fuse the fronts, facings, under collar, hems, and sleeve hems with weft-insertion fusible.

Modern "speed tailoring" replaces the hand-padded chest piece with layered fusibles — full hand tailoring is a separate rabbit hole.

2Shell details flat
  1. Sew darts or princess seams in the fronts.
  2. Welt or patch pockets while the fronts are still flat pieces on the table.
3Shell assembly
  1. Join the fronts to the back at the shoulders and side seams.
  2. Press over a ham and seam roll as you go — pressing builds the jacket's shape.
4Notched collar

The under collar attaches to the jacket's neckline; the upper collar attaches to the facings.

Then the two assemblies are joined in one continuous seam up one lapel, around the collar, down the other lapel. Grade the allowances, clip the corner at the notch, press hard (technique below).

5Sleeves in the round
  1. Set two-piece sleeves in the round, caps eased.
  2. Sleeve heads and shoulder pads sewn in by hand.

These two pieces of internal padding are most of what makes a blazer look tailored.

6Lining unit

Assemble the whole lining as one piece — including the center-back ease pleat — plus the facings.

7Bag the lining
  1. Machine-sew the lining to the jacket around every edge — facings, back neck, hems.
  2. Pull it right side out through a gap left in one lining sleeve seam (technique below).
8Buttonholes, buttons, final press
  • Keyhole buttonholes if your machine does them.
  • A hard final press with a clapper sets everything.

Key Techniques

The notched collar

The under collar attaches to the jacket's neckline; the upper collar attaches to the front facings. Then the two assemblies are joined in one continuous seam up one lapel, around the collar, down the other lapel. Grade the seam allowances, clip the corner at the notch, and press hard. The key fit detail: the upper collar and the lapel side that shows must be fractionally larger (turn of cloth), and the roll line should be pressed in — not creased — over a ham.

Bagging the lining

The commercial method that eliminates nearly all hand sewing:

  1. Sew the lining+facing unit to the jacket around the facings and back neck.
  2. Sew the jacket hem to the lining hem, right sides together (this feels wrong; trust it).
  3. Sew each sleeve hem to its lining sleeve.
  4. Pull the whole jacket through the gap you left in one lining sleeve seam, then close the gap.

It feels like magic the first time. Grainline's tutorial is the cleanest explanation of the concept; Closet Core's Clare and Kelly sewalongs show it on a real coat and anorak with photos at every step.

Pressing equipment matters here

A tailor's ham (curved seams, darts, collar roll), seam roll (sleeve seams without imprints), clapper (crisp wool edges — press with steam, slam the clapper on, hold until cool), and point presser (collar points). For wool, you press more than you sew.

Hong Kong finish (unlined jackets)

If you skip the lining, bind every visible seam allowance in bias strips — slower than lining, but the inside becomes a feature.

Serger Notes

  • Lined jackets: serging is mostly unnecessary (everything is enclosed), but serging the hem edges of loosely woven wool stops fraying during construction.
  • Unlined / casual jackets: serge or Hong Kong every exposed allowance.
